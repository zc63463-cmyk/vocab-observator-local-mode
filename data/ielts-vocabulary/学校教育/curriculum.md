# curriculum

> [!info] 基础信息
> **音标** /kəˈrɪkjələm/ | **词级** 雅思词汇 | **语义场** [[教育与课程相关]] | **CERF** B2

## 核心释义

**n.** ①==**课程体系**==（学校教授的所有科目的总和）`national curriculum` [教育]；②==**课程设置**==（特定学校或专业的课程安排）`school curriculum` [教育]；③==**课程内容**==（特定科目的教学内容）`mathematics curriculum` [教育]；

> [!tip] 原型义
> **原型义**：学校教授科目的总和——课程体系的完整框架
> **延伸维度**：知识路径 → 系统路径
> **隐喻类型**：结构隐喻（"奔跑的路径" → "学习的路径"——从物理路径映射到知识路径）

## 词根词缀

源自拉丁语 **curriculum**（跑道、课程），来自 **currere**（奔跑）。与 **current**（当前的）、**course**（课程）、**cursor**（光标）同源。词根 *curr-* 在拉丁语中本指"奔跑"，暗示一种"沿着路径前进"的学习过程——课程就是学生在知识道路上的"跑道"。

**叙事**：curriculum 的原始画面是古罗马的竞技场跑道（curriculum），运动员沿着跑道奔跑。这种"沿着路径前进"的概念后来被借用到教育领域——课程就是学生在知识道路上的"跑道"，从初级到高级，沿着设计好的路径前进。如今，curriculum 指代整个课程体系，包括所有科目的设置和安排。

>    - → **国家课程**：全国统一的课程标准

>      - 激活条件：engineering curriculum, medical curriculum
>    - → **课程内容**：特定科目的教学内容
>      - 激活条件：mathematics curriculum, science curriculum

## 搭配与短语

- **national curriculum**：国家课程
  - *The national curriculum sets standards for all schools in the country.*
- **school curriculum**：学校课程
  - *The school curriculum includes both academic and practical subjects.*
- **core curriculum**：核心课程
  - *Mathematics and science are part of the core curriculum.*
- **curriculum design/development**：课程设计/开发
  - *Curriculum design is a complex process that involves many stakeholders.*
- **hidden curriculum**：隐性课程
  - *The hidden curriculum includes the values and norms students learn outside of formal lessons.*

## 真题/语料关联

- **雅思写作 Task 2（教育类）**：*Some people think that the school curriculum should be more practical and less theoretical.*
  - 可用 curriculum 讨论课程设置：*A well-designed curriculum should balance theoretical knowledge and practical skills.*
- **雅思阅读学术语料**：*The curriculum has been revised to include more interdisciplinary courses.*
- **雅思口语 Part 3**：*What changes would you make to the school curriculum?*
  - *I would add more creative subjects to the curriculum to encourage innovation.*
