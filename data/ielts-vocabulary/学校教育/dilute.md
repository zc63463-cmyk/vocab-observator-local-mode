# dilute

> [!info] 基础信息
> **音标** /daɪˈluːt/ | **词级** 雅思词汇 | **语义场** [[学科、数学与科学术语]] | **CERF** B2

## 核心释义

**v.** ①==**稀释**==（加入溶剂降低浓度）`dilute the solution` [化学/通用]；②==**削弱**==（降低效果、力量或价值）`dilute the impact` [通用]；③==**淡化**==（使颜色或味道变淡）`dilute the color` [艺术/通用]；
**adj.** ①==**稀释的**==（浓度降低的）`dilute acid` [化学]；②==**淡的**==（浓度低的）`dilute solution` [化学]；

> [!tip] 原型义
> **原型义**：稀释——加入溶剂降低浓度
> **延伸维度**：化学路径 → 抽象路径
> **隐喻类型**：隐喻延伸（"化学稀释" → "效果削弱"——从浓度降低映射到效果减弱）

## 词根词缀

源自拉丁语 **diluere**（洗掉），由 **dis-**（离开）和 **lavare**（洗）组成。与 **dilution**（稀释）、**diluent**（稀释剂）同源。前缀 *dis-* 表示"离开"，*lavare* 表示"洗"，合起来就是"洗掉"。

**叙事**：dilute 的原始画面是往浓缩液中加水，浓度逐渐降低。这种"稀释"不仅是化学变化，更是效果变化——政策的效果像溶液一样被"稀释"削弱。后来，"dilute"成为描述"稀释"和"削弱"的通用词汇。

> [!check]- 链路验证

## 搭配与短语

- **dilute the solution**：稀释溶液
  - *You should dilute the solution with water before use.*
- **dilute the impact**：削弱影响
  - *The new policy may dilute the impact of the reforms.*
- **dilute the color**：淡化颜色
  - *Adding white paint will dilute the color.*
- **dilute acid**：稀酸
  - *Dilute acid is safer to handle than concentrated acid.*
- **dilute with water**：用水稀释
  - *Dilute the juice with water to make it less sweet.*

## 真题/语料关联

- **雅思阅读化学语料**：*To dilute a solution, add more solvent to decrease the concentration.*
- **雅思写作 Task 2（政府类）**：*Some people believe that new laws dilute existing protections.*
  - 可用 dilute 讨论法律削弱：*The new legislation may dilute the effectiveness of environmental regulations.*
- **雅思口语 Part 3**：*How do you make strong coffee weaker?*
  - *You can dilute it with hot water to reduce the strength.*

| 搭配 | dilute solution, dilute acid | weaken the argument, weaken the economy | thin the paint, thin the mixture |
