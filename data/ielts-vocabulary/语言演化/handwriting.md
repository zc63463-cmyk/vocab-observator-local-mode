# handwriting

> [!info] 基础信息
> **音标** /ˈændraɪtɪŋ/ | **词级** 雅思词汇 | **语义场** [[语言与符号基础]] | **CERF** B2

## 核心释义

**n.** ①==**笔迹**==（某人写字的独特风格）`recognize someone's handwriting` [通用]；②==**书法**==（书写的艺术或技巧）`neat handwriting` [通用]；③==**手写**==（用手书写的行为）`handwriting practice` [通用]；④==**手稿**==（手写的文件）`handwriting sample` [通用/法律]；

> [!tip] 原型义
> **原型义**：用手握住笔，在纸上留下字迹
> **延伸维度**：书写路径 + 个人路径 + 文化路径
> **隐喻类型**：转喻（书写动作 → 书写结果）

## 词根词缀

由 **hand**（手）+ **writing**（书写）构成的复合词，字面意思就是"手写的文字"。这种构词方式直接反映了 handwriting 的本质：它是用手完成的书写——区别于印刷或打字。同源词：**handwritten**（手写的）、**write**（写）、**writer**（作者）。

**叙事**：handwriting 的原始画面是一个人坐在书桌前，手握毛笔，在宣纸上一笔一画地写字。handwriting 不只是"写字"，而是一种"个人印记"——每个人的 handwriting 都独一无二，就像指纹一样。笔迹能反映一个人的性格：字迹工整的人通常做事认真，字迹潦草的人可能思维敏捷。在数字时代，handwriting 正在消失，但它依然是人类文明的重要遗产。

## 搭配与短语

- **neat handwriting**：字迹工整
  - *She has very neat handwriting that is easy to read.*
- **illegible handwriting**：难以辨认的笔迹
  - *The doctor's illegible handwriting made the prescription hard to read.*
- **handwriting analysis**：笔迹分析
  - *Handwriting analysis can reveal aspects of a person's personality.*
- **handwriting recognition**：手写识别
  - *Modern tablets have advanced handwriting recognition technology.*
- **by hand / in handwriting**：手写的
  - *The letter was written by hand in beautiful cursive script.*

## 真题/语料关联

- **雅思阅读学术语料**：*Studies have shown that handwriting activates regions of the brain associated with learning and memory.*
- **雅思写作 Task 2（教育类）**：*Some people believe that handwriting is no longer necessary in the digital age. To what extent do you agree?*
  - 可用 handwriting 讨论传统技能：*While typing is more efficient, handwriting helps develop fine motor skills and cognitive abilities.*
- **雅思口语 Part 3**：*Do you think handwriting will disappear in the future?*
  - *I think handwriting will become less common, but it won't completely disappear because many people still value the personal touch of a handwritten note.*

## 同义词辨析

| 维度 | handwriting | calligraphy | script |
|------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| 核心义 | 笔迹（个人书写） | 书法（艺术书写） | 字体（书写风格） |
| 语域 | 通用 | 艺术/正式 | 通用/技术 |
| 搭配 | neat handwriting, someone's handwriting | Chinese calligraphy, practice calligraphy | cursive script, printed script |
| 情感 | 含个人/日常感 | 含艺术/高雅感 | 含技术/正式感 |
| 差异 | 强调**个人风格** | 强调**艺术技巧** | 强调**字体形式** |

**雅思同义替换提示**：handwriting = penmanship（书法）；handwritten = by hand；good handwriting = neat writing。
