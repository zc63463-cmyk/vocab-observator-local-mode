# disseminate

> [!info] 基础信息
> **音标** /dɪˈsemɪneɪt/ | **词级** 雅思词汇 | **语义场** [[媒体与传播]] | **CERF** C1

## 核心释义

**v.** ①==**传播，散布**==（将信息或知识广泛传播给众人）`disseminate information` [学术/通用]；②==**推广**==（将理念或方法推广到更广泛的范围）`disseminate knowledge` [学术]；③==**扩散**==（使某物分散到各处）`disseminate ideas` [通用]；

> [!tip] 原型义
> **原型义**：将信息或知识广泛传播给众人
> **延伸维度**：知识传播 → 信息扩散 → 理念推广
> **隐喻类型**：隐喻（"播种" → "传播信息"——将信息像种子一样撒播出去）

## 词根词缀

源自拉丁语 **disseminare**（撒播种子），由 **dis-**（分散）+ **seminare**（播种）构成。seminare 与 **seminar**（研讨会）、**seed**（种子）同源。词根 *seminare* 在拉丁语中本指"播种"，暗示一种"分散传播"的动作。

**叙事**：disseminate 的原始画面是一个农夫在田地里撒播种子。后来，人们发现"传播信息"就像"播种"——将信息像种子一样撒播出去，让它在各处生根发芽。disseminate 从"播种"的字面义演变为"传播信息"的比喻义。

## 搭配与短语

- **disseminate information**：传播信息
  - *The organization aims to disseminate information about health and safety.*
- **disseminate knowledge**：传播知识
  - *Universities play a key role in disseminating knowledge.*
- **disseminate ideas**：传播理念
  - *The internet has made it easier to disseminate ideas.*
- **disseminate widely**：广泛传播
  - *The report was disseminated widely through social media.*
- **be disseminated through**：通过……传播
  - *The news was disseminated through various channels.*

## 真题/语料关联

- **雅思阅读学术语料**：*The research findings were disseminated through academic journals and conferences.*
- **雅思写作 Task 2（科技类）**：*Some people believe that the internet has made it easier to disseminate information.*
  - 可用 disseminate 讨论"信息传播"：*The internet has revolutionized the way we disseminate information, making it possible to reach a global audience instantly.*
- **雅思口语 Part 3**：*How has technology changed the way information is disseminated?*
  - *Technology has made it much easier to disseminate information quickly and widely.*

| 语域 | 正式/学术 | 通用 | 通用/商业 |

## 记忆锚点

**谐音锚点**：disseminate = "弟色米内特" → 弟弟在颜色米里内特撒播 → disseminate = 撒播/传播

**画面锚点**：想象一个农夫在田地里撒播（disseminate）种子。

**词根锚点**：dis（分散）+ semin（种子）+ ate（动词后缀） → 撒播种子 → 传播。
