# leaflet

> [!info] 基础信息
> **音标** /ˈliːflɪt/ | **词级** 雅思词汇 | **语义场** [[媒体与传播]] | **CERF** B1

## 核心释义

**n.** ①==**传单**==（用于宣传或广告的小型印刷品）`an advertising leaflet` [宣传]；②==**小册子**==（介绍产品或服务的小型手册）`an information leaflet` [宣传/商业]；③==**小叶**==（植物学中的小叶片）`a small leaflet` [植物学]；

> [!tip] 原型义
> **原型义**：用于宣传或介绍的小型印刷品
> **延伸维度**：宣传传单 → 信息手册 → 广告材料
> **隐喻类型**：字面义（"小叶子" → 像叶子一样的小纸片）

## 词根词缀

由 **leaf**（叶子）+ **-let**（表示"小"的后缀）构成。leaf 源自古英语 *lēaf*（叶子、纸张），-let 源自古法语 *-let*（小），如 booklet（小册子）、piglet（小猪）。复合词 leaflet 的字面意思是"小叶子"，后特指"像叶子一样的小纸片"，即"传单"。

**叙事**：leaflet 的原始画面是一片小叶子飘落在地上。后来，人们发现"传单"就像"小叶子"一样——轻薄、易分发、数量众多。leaflet 从"小叶子"的字面义演变为"传单"的比喻义。

## 搭配与短语

- **an advertising leaflet**：广告传单
  - *The company distributed thousands of advertising leaflets.*
- **a promotional leaflet**：宣传传单
  - *The promotional leaflet highlighted the benefits of the new product.*
- **an information leaflet**：信息手册
  - *The tourist information office provides free leaflets about local attractions.*
- **a travel leaflet**：旅游手册
  - *I picked up a travel leaflet at the hotel reception.*
- **distribute/hand out leaflets**：分发传单
  - *Volunteers were handing out leaflets in the street.*

## 真题/语料关联

- **雅思阅读学术语料**：*The leaflet provided detailed information about the new recycling program.*
- **雅思写作 Task 2（环境类）**：*Some people believe that distributing leaflets is an effective way to raise awareness about environmental issues.*
  - 可用 leaflet 讨论"环保宣传"：*Leaflets can be an effective tool for raising awareness, but they must be well-designed and informative.*
- **雅思口语 Part 2**：*Describe a time when you received a leaflet.*
  - *I received a leaflet about a new restaurant opening in my neighborhood.*

| 语域 | 通用/宣传 | 正式/宣传 | 商业/旅游 |
| 搭配 | advertising leaflet | information pamphlet | travel brochure |
| 情感 | 中性 | 中性 | 中性 |

**雅思同义替换提示**：leaflet = flyer = handout；information leaflet = information sheet。

## 记忆锚点

**谐音锚点**：leaflet = "离离弗特" → 离离原上草，弗特像叶子 → leaflet = 小叶子/传单

**画面锚点**：想象一片小叶子（leaflet）飘落在地上，就像一张传单。

**词根锚点**：leaf（叶子）+ let（小） → 小叶子 → 传单。
